Marvel Avengers - Complete Reference Document

=== OVERVIEW ===
The Avengers are a team of superheroes appearing in Marvel Comics and the Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU).
They were first assembled to fight threats too large for any single hero to handle.
The team was founded by Nick Fury, director of S.H.I.E.L.D., using the Avengers Initiative.

=== CORE MEMBERS ===

Iron Man (Tony Stark)
- Real Name: Tony Stark
- Abilities: Genius-level intellect, powered armor suit (Iron Man suit), flight, energy repulsors, missiles
- Background: Billionaire industrialist and inventor. CEO of Stark Industries.
- Key Events: Built the first Iron Man suit in a cave to escape captivity. Later upgraded to Mark 85 armor.
- Famous Quote: "I am Iron Man."
- Status: Sacrificed himself using the Infinity Gauntlet to defeat Thanos in Avengers: Endgame.

Captain America (Steve Rogers)
- Real Name: Steve Rogers
- Abilities: Super soldier serum enhanced strength, speed, agility, and healing. Vibranium shield.
- Background: A frail young man from Brooklyn who was transformed into a super soldier during World War II.
- Key Events: Frozen in ice for 70 years. Revived in modern times. Led the Avengers in multiple battles.
- Famous Quote: "I can do this all day."
- Status: Traveled back in time to live out his life. Passed the shield to Sam Wilson (Falcon).

Thor Odinson
- Real Name: Thor Odinson
- Abilities: God of Thunder, controls lightning, superhuman strength, Mjolnir (enchanted hammer), Stormbreaker axe.
- Background: Prince of Asgard, son of Odin. Banished to Earth and learned humility.
- Key Events: Fought Loki multiple times. Survived Thanos's attack. Killed Thanos in Endgame (but too late).
- Famous Quote: "I'm still worthy."
- Status: Joined the Guardians of the Galaxy after Endgame.

Hulk (Bruce Banner)
- Real Name: Bruce Banner
- Abilities: Unlimited strength (increases with anger), near invulnerability, regeneration.
- Background: Scientist exposed to gamma radiation. Transforms into the Hulk when angry.
- Key Events: Merged his Banner intelligence with Hulk's body (Smart Hulk). Used the Infinity Gauntlet to bring back the snapped people.
- Famous Quote: "Hulk Smash!"
- Status: Active. Arm permanently damaged after using the Infinity Gauntlet.

Black Widow (Natasha Romanoff)
- Real Name: Natasha Romanoff
- Abilities: Master spy, martial arts expert, acrobatics, Widow's Bite (electroshock weapons).
- Background: Former KGB agent trained in the Red Room. Defected to S.H.I.E.L.D.
- Key Events: Sacrificed herself on Vormir so Hawkeye could obtain the Soul Stone.
- Famous Quote: "Whatever it takes."
- Status: Deceased. Sacrificed for the Soul Stone.

Hawkeye (Clint Barton)
- Real Name: Clint Barton
- Abilities: Master archer, expert marksman, hand-to-hand combat.
- Background: S.H.I.E.L.D. agent. Family man with wife and three children.
- Key Events: Was snapped away with his family. Became Ronin after losing his family. Retrieved the Soul Stone with Black Widow.
- Status: Retired. Passed the Hawkeye mantle to Kate Bishop.

=== VILLAINS ===

Thanos
- Title: The Mad Titan
- Abilities: Superhuman strength, durability, energy manipulation, telepathy, matter manipulation (with Infinity Gauntlet).
- Goal: Collect all 6 Infinity Stones to wipe out half of all life in the universe to prevent overpopulation.
- Key Events: Snapped half of all life in Avengers: Infinity War. Destroyed the Infinity Stones. Killed by Thor. Past version defeated by Iron Man's snap in Endgame.
- Famous Quote: "I am inevitable."

Loki
- Title: God of Mischief
- Abilities: Sorcery, illusion casting, shapeshifting, Asgardian strength.
- Background: Adopted brother of Thor. Son of Frost Giant king Laufey, raised by Odin.
- Key Events: Led the Chitauri invasion of New York. Helped Thor multiple times. Killed by Thanos. Alternate timeline Loki escaped with the Tesseract.

Ultron
- Created by: Tony Stark and Bruce Banner
- Abilities: Artificial intelligence, can transfer consciousness, controls machines, vibranium body.
- Goal: Believed humanity was the greatest threat to Earth and sought to cause extinction-level event.
- Key Events: Created Vision accidentally. Defeated by the Avengers in Sokovia.

=== INFINITY STONES ===
1. Space Stone (Tesseract) - Blue - Controls space and teleportation
2. Mind Stone (in Vision's forehead) - Yellow - Controls minds
3. Reality Stone (Aether) - Red - Alters reality
4. Power Stone - Purple - Unlimited energy and destruction
5. Time Stone (Eye of Agamotto) - Green - Controls time
6. Soul Stone (on Vormir) - Orange - Controls souls

=== KEY EVENTS TIMELINE ===
- 2012: Battle of New York (Avengers vs Chitauri)
- 2015: Ultron incident, Vision created, Sokovia destroyed
- 2016: Avengers Civil War - Team splits over Sokovia Accords
- 2018: Thanos collects all stones, snaps half of life (The Decimation)
- 2023: Time Heist - Avengers travel back in time to collect stones
- 2023: Final Battle - Iron Man snaps Thanos and his army out of existence

=== TEAMS AND GROUPS ===
- Original Avengers: Iron Man, Captain America, Thor, Hulk, Black Widow, Hawkeye
- New Avengers: Falcon, War Machine, Vision, Scarlet Witch, Spider-Man, Black Panther, Doctor Strange
- Guardians of the Galaxy: Star-Lord, Gamora, Rocket, Groot, Drax, Mantis, Nebula
- S.H.I.E.L.D.: Nick Fury, Maria Hill, Phil Coulson

=== TECHNOLOGY ===
- Iron Man Suits: Mark 1 to Mark 85, each with upgraded capabilities
- Vibranium: Near-indestructible metal from Wakanda, used in Captain America's shield and Black Panther's suit
- Pym Particles: Allow objects and people to shrink or grow (used by Ant-Man)
- Time Machine (Quantum Tunnel): Built by Tony Stark to enable time travel via the Quantum Realm
